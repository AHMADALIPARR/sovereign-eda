- Requirements change through requirements/ files, with a traceability
  row for every new requirement. A requirement with no trace entry
  fails the gate.
- Defects are recorded in assurance/defects/defect-log.md with an ID,
  a finding date, and a status. A defect is closed by a fix and a
  re-run, not by editing the log.
- The watermark is re-chained on every commit that adds or changes a
  file. See WATERMARK.md. The gate recomputes the chain.
- The toolchain record is updated whenever a tool is installed,
  removed, or replaced, from measured output.
- The release-readiness report is regenerated by the gate on every
  run. It is not edited by hand to pass.
- The board and the Malbolge circuit are maintained as one monorepo.
  Layout changes need the owner's explicit direction (ADR-001).
